Recitation 1 of 108 · Ṛgveda 7.59.12
“नाम” इति व्यक्तये मोक्षप्राप्त्यर्थं सङ्कल्पः
त्र्य॑म्बकं यजामहे सु॒गन्धिं॑ पुष्टि॒वर्ध॑नम् ।
उ॒र्वा॒रु॒कमि॑व॒ बन्ध॑नान्मृ॒त्योर्मु॑क्षीय॒मामृता॑त् ॥
tryambakaṃ yajāmahe sugandhiṃ puṣṭivardhanam | urvārukam iva bandhanān mṛtyor mukṣīya māmṛtāt ||
Plain sense (one rendering): We worship the three-eyed one, fragrant and life-nourishing. As a cucumber is released from its stem, may I be released from death, but not from immortality.
